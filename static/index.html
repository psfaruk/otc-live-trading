The honest math

Signals are emitted only when several independent strategies (STAT, REGIME, POSITION, PATTERN, STATE, FLOW) agree with enough weight and no veto. The measured edge on the calibrated backtest is ~53-54% — above the ~52.1% break-even at a 92% payout, but nothing close to a guarantee. Anything promising 80-90% on a 1-minute OTC feed is selling you something. Risk 1-2% per trade, no martingale.
